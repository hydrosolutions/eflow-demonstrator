Overall judgement
The demonstrator is a useful, honest operational illustration: it makes allocation priorities, reservoir routing, drainage returns and method-component differences visible. It is not yet a self-sufficient illustration of the complete report procedure. The principal weaknesses are method scope and missing decision stages, not evidence that its routed flows are fabricated. Keep the current prototype and its useful comparison; repair the scope boundaries rather than rebuild the interface.

PRIORITY FINDINGS

R1 — P1: the five-year recommendation is no longer consistent across the live report.
UI: P/calculated-view.js:96, “Annual record maintenance; proposed full review within five years, or earlier when evidence changes.”
Current report: part_computational_requirements.qmd:106 explicitly proposes annual review and fuller reassessment with the THREE-year basin-plan review; appendix_computational_requirements.qmd:61 repeats it. In contrast, part4_recommended_method.qmd:675 and part5_implementation_governance.qmd:923 still state a FIVE-year plan-related cycle. Appendix A, appendices.qmd:1840–1851, ties reassessment to plan renewal plus intervening triggers rather than resolving this number.
Classification: actual conflict among live report sources, carried into the UI; not merely an omitted detail. The new computational chapter cites Code Article 30, but I have not independently revalidated that legal citation in this review.
Action: report owner reconciles these live passages; the demonstrator should use the resolved cadence. Pending that, “Annual review; fuller reassessment with the basin-plan review or earlier material change” avoids silently choosing between incompatible live instructions. Do not present five years as settled report fidelity.

R2 — P1: the Swiss floor is promoted to a delivery instruction without showing the report's separate branch.
UI: P/calculated-view.js:43 gives one target-to-instruction rule for all methods; :96 displays Candidate → Operating instruction → Modelled delivery and delivery-shortfall counts for Swiss as for the other options. P/targets.json:2270 names this “illustrative_floor_operating_target”, while :2309 correctly retains formalEntryStatus=pending. Live Swiss profiles do contain both candidate_litres and issued_litres.
Report: appendices.qmd:1588–1591 explicitly says a floor-only branch keeps regime, requirement and delivery obligation pending and issues NO delivery verdict from the floor; :1640–1643 repeats this for entry. part_computational_requirements.qmd:102 and appendix_computational_requirements.qmd:52,71 expressly preserve that distinction. The separate floor prohibition also needs its own cause/evidence test (appendices.qmd:1795–1814).
Classification: deliberate hypothetical operational extension, not the report's entry-route procedure. Existing “hypothetical” and no-official-compliance caveats reduce the risk, but do not explain the particular methodological departure.
Action: keep routing the Swiss number if desired, but label it “floor used here as a hypothetical operating target”; show report delivery obligation and floor-prohibition finding as pending/not tested. One compact method-status note is enough; do not invent the missing official obligation or call the numerical red light a report floor breach.

Add “The evidence selects the applicable route; these buttons compare examples.” Keep the current assumptions; do not fabricate missing components to make the routes look complete or use downstream volume to rank ecological adequacy.

FAITHFUL / ACCEPTABLE SIMPLIFICATIONS TO KEEP
- Swiss: assumed 1.95 m3/s is expressly NOT daily-estimated Q347; the actual illustrative Fishy graduated curve is disclosed as neither a literal Swiss statutory table nor adopted Uzbek coefficients (P/calculated-view.js:6,77,82,85). This is compatible with conditional illustration and avoids misusing coarse monthly repetition as validated daily evidence. The absence of additions is acknowledged. It does not demonstrate statutory Swiss values.
- Kazakh: normal50→75 and dry95→97 preserve the report's shift directions; UI :82 explicitly says percentages are annual exceedance classes, not shares of today's inflow. Class assignment and Q75=.8Q50 / Q95=.5Q50 / Q97=.4Q50 are assumptions, not statistical inferences (:17,:85 and target limitations). The common mean-one calendar shape is a declared simplification, not the report's shifted-class, melt-aligned historical shape (appendices.qmd:1060–1098). No new decision is needed merely to retain these existing toy assumptions.
- Snow trout: source baseflows 0.3/0.5/0.4 remain unscaled, and exact source WUA points are shown without interpolation or a locally evaluated habitat claim (:7,:75,:85). Source river matches Koksu but network geometry is still hypothetical. Retain the explicit exclusion of source spill/pipe rules. It is one habitat-derived baseflow component, not the whole top-tier method.
- Objects/topology: natural river, artificial canal/drain, modified-natural reservoir location and independent wetland receptor are distinguished; junctions are not water bodies and calculation sections are not automatically new water bodies (P/app.js:14–17). Unknown wetland connection is not fabricated.
- Operations: fixed target/input under alternative priorities and separate R2a/R2b checks make return-flow masking visible. A green R2b never establishes R2a success. This follows the report's location-specific testing and no whole-body adequacy shortcut.
- Numerical meaning: model monthly means are labelled, daily ledgers exist, native routing and Fishy checking are distinguished from ecological validation. Source/code/target provenance is retained. Ordinary water closure is not sold as habitat or legal compliance (P/index.html:16; calculated-view.js:85,:96).
