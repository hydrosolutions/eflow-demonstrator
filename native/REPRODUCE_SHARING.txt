Additive drought-sharing native replay

Run commands from native/. The original guided_model.py, guided.json and guided-data.js
remain immutable. sharing_model.py imports their admitted scalar constants, forcing and
unchanged natural-loss, irrigation and reservoir rules; it does not monkeypatch them.
Its separate model/Fishy adapter records the new scenario and issued-policy identity.
The parent ecological schedule retains guided-policy-v1. The sharing instruction uses
guided-sharing-20pct-v1 and the dataset is guided-sharing-synthetic-v1.

Policy and numerical representation
Offer at most 20% of the river after the retained 5% natural exchange, limited by the
existing seasonal request and actual available water. The offered cap is rounded DOWN
to whole 2 m3/day before native execution. Thus cap reduction is less than 2 m3/day;
the rule never exceeds 20%. Half the actual irrigation diversion is consumed, half
returns on the same day, carrying all diverted salt. The same original synthetic drought,
reference, ecological target, reservoir and spatial mapping remain in use. No domestic
or industrial demand or protected-use ranking is represented. This is an illustrative
allocation comparison, not an adopted priority, recommended percentage or safety floor.

The prospective cap is derived from the hashed frozen source forcing; changing that
forcing requires regeneration and admission. Native execution also checks actual water
availability. Every actual inflow and transfer is independently checked in integer litres;
no tolerance hides changes in the quantized policy. Ecological and issued findings are
separate. A met instruction does not erase ecological deficit.

One initial annual attempt exposed a one-litre difference at day index94 when using a
dynamic real-valued divide-by-five branch directly. Native balances still closed, but the
exact intended-policy oracle rejected the result. The failed source and physical evidence
are preserved in the audit's failed-attempt-01.zip. The downward whole-2m3 cap is the
explicit representation repair, independently admitted before the second/final annual
attempt. Old results were not rewritten. The admitted output folder's attempt-02 suffix
records this history; reproducing the final implementation needs only one fresh run.
