6–10 minutes · The same place, dates and forcing

What changes when we remove management?

Remove or replace

Artificial abstraction, irrigation use and associated returns. Replace constructed reservoir regulation with the declared natural river counterpart.

Retain

The same forcing and an assumed 5% natural channel exchange. The natural counterpart is memoryless; no lateral inflow or groundwater physics is inferred.

Account for

Initial and final storage, consumption, outlet export and losses. Artificial stored water is not emptied into the natural scenario.

This is a synthetic natural-system scenario. Taqsim accounts for the supplied system; it does not discover a natural reference from an observed gauge.
